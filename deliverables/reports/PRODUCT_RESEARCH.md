# Pawverio product research

Research date: **4 November 2026** (Europe/London). This is a limited desk-research pass using publisher-owned product pages and support documentation. It is not an installed-app benchmark, user study, pricing survey, trademark clearance or an exhaustive competitor census.

## Pawverio differentiation hypotheses

The following are **hypotheses**, not verified demand or unique-market claims:

1. **A Fire tablet as a household care station.** A well-spaced adaptive interface may suit a kitchen or shared family device better than a phone-oriented journal. Validate on physical 7–10-inch Fire tablets in both orientations; compare task-entry and completion speed with participants’ current method.
2. **Low-friction, local ownership.** No mandatory account and local records may appeal to owners who prefer a private organiser. Validate whether users can add their first pet and reminder without help, understand backup limitations, and recover their records from an encrypted backup.
3. **Accessible multilingual commands with honest fallbacks.** Push-to-talk plus text commands could reduce repeated navigation. Installed speech services vary on Fire hardware, so voice availability is a technical assumption until tested per device and language. Measure successful intended actions, false actions, denied permissions and fallback completion.
4. **Clear one-time upgrade.** The brief’s proposed £4.99 one-time price is an unvalidated test-price hypothesis. Use actual localized Amazon product prices in the app. Validate product comprehension and purchase/restore flows in Amazon’s test environment; do not infer willingness to pay from competitor feature lists.
5. **Readable sitter handover.** A private, explicit-confirmation export may help owners hand over daily instructions without adding another household member to an online service. Validate whether a sitter can find meal, medication, vet and emergency information accurately in the exported file.

Amazon’s official announcement ends its Appstore support for generic Android mobile devices from 20 August 2025 while retaining Fire tablet support. Thus the primary store opportunity in this project is compatible Fire tablets; ordinary Android distribution is separate. This is a distribution constraint, not evidence of an underserved or profitable market. [Amazon announcement](https://developer.amazon.com/apps-and-games/blogs/2025/02/upcoming-changes-to-amazon-appstore-for-android-devices-and-coins-program).

## Practical validation priorities

Use a small moderated study with dog owners, cat owners, a multi-pet household and a sitter; include a tablet user and accessibility needs where feasible. Test first-pet setup, a repeating meal reminder, completing and undoing care, a medication entry copied from an existing prescription, and a sitter export. Use participants’ own task descriptions without collecting unnecessary private records. Fictional health data is sufficient for the study.

Record observed completion rate, time, errors and misunderstandings. Obtain explicit consent for any feedback collection. Compare these observations with acceptance criteria before adding features. A local-only product need not add a tracking SDK merely to perform this validation. Commercial demand, retention and price acceptance remain **NOT TESTED** until evidence exists.
